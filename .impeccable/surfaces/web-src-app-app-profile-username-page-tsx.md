## Scope and mode

Operate mode across the authenticated product shell, anchored by `web/src/app/(app)/profile/[username]/page.tsx` and inherited by feed, chats, username market, gifts, discover, notifications, and settings.

## Audience, job, and action

Mobile-first social users scan identity, decide whether to converse, privately remember context, and understand username or gift ownership. Primary actions are message, note, reserve/list, and transfer; ownership state must remain explicit.

## Chosen direction

Rounded identity network, grounded direction six from concept seed `3c690579`, pinned by the user's Telegram-like product references. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/profile-option-b-approved.png`. The memorable moment is an edition-marked featured collectible integrated into the cover while identity data remains dense and immediate.

## Implementation inventory

- Navigation: semantic links with Lucide icons; five-action mobile dock and restrained desktop channel rail.
- Typography: Onest Variable for brand, display, Cyrillic UI, and body copy.
- Surfaces: graphite matte fields, 1px recessed hairlines, 18–30px rounded groups, restrained offset depth; pills only for compact state.
- State: violet for primary/selected action, cyan for links, amber for provenance/rarity, green for presence, and labels/icons accompanying color.
- Media: avatar and cover photography; generated 1:1 raster gift objects; core controls and text remain semantic code.
- Motion: small spatial/state transitions and a guarded collectible float, disabled under reduced motion.
- Responsive: edge-to-edge mobile channel below 900px; compact rail at 900px, full rail at 1180px, context rail at 1420px; no horizontal content overflow.

## Unresolved production decisions

Real payment settlement, blockchain minting, moderation operations, email delivery, and production object storage are outside the local MVP.
